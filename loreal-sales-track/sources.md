# Source Register

**How this research was done (read this first).** The research for this folder was run on 30 Oct 2026 using web search. Direct page fetching was **blocked** by the session's network policy. So every claim below was checked against search-result summaries of the named page, not a full read of the document. Before any number goes on a slide, open the primary URL and confirm it. The "Verify before deck" column marks the claims that matter most.

**Reliability grades**
- **A**: primary source (L'Oréal filing or website, regulator, company press release)
- **B**: reputable press or brokerage research quoting a primary source
- **C**: vendor or practitioner blog. Use only as an illustration, never as evidence.

**Data scope tags:** `[IN]` is India-specific data. `[GL]` is global or group-level data. `[CASE]` comes from the case PDF.

## What we could NOT find publicly (and must not claim)
- L'Oréal India's channel mix (% of sales by GT, MT, e-com, q-comm, salon, pharmacy).
- L'Oréal India's distributor count, outlet reach, field-force size or beat frequency.
- Which SFA/DMS L'Oréal India uses, and whether it captures retailer stock or batch expiry.
- L'Oréal India's return policy terms, including what "capped at ~1.5%" means in practice.
- Any published L'Oréal India figure on expired or returned stock beyond the case's ~1.5%.
- Independent (non-vendor) studies measuring the expiry reduction from field-sales interventions.

These gaps are a strength in the pitch if handled honestly. They become **questions we ask L'Oréal during the pilot** (see `10-kpis-pilot.md`), not numbers we invent.
