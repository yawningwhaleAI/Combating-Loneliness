# 11 · Slide-by-Slide Presentation Blueprint

**Format recommendation:** **12 core slides + a 3-minute live demo + 6 appendix slides.** That fits a ~12–15 minute slot; confirm the competition's time limit. Every title is an *action title* (the claim), not a topic. A small **"Fact / Assumption"** legend sits in the footer of every number-bearing slide.

**Storyline in one breath:** *Waste is decided when the order is taken and discovered when the return arrives. The rep is in the shop many times in between. We give them the signals, the playbook and the incentive to act early, with a light tool that turns it into a measurable, rupee-tracked routine. Less waste, no lost growth, proven in a 16-week pilot.*

## Design guidance
- **One idea per slide**; max ~30 words of body text; numbers in big type with their source tag.
- **Colour code = risk states**, used consistently: 🔴 near-expiry, 🟠 ageing, 🔵 stock-out, 🟡 overstock, 🟢 healthy. The app uses the same palette.
- Use **real photos you take yourselves** in local stores (dusty shelf, cartons) instead of stock images. It signals field work. Don't photograph identifiable people or private store data without permission.
- Avoid: "synergy", "leverage", "holistic", "AI-powered" in titles. Prefer verbs and rupees.
- Put **"Illustrative"** watermarks on every chart that uses assumptions.
